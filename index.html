About Pauline

After 22 years living abroad in the UK, Morocco, Sri Lanka, Japan, Thailand and Australia, I moved back to France where I grew up.
And I had to start from scratch. I have missed the cheese and the wine but I have not missed the complexity of the system.

Knowing the excitement of a new start, I am also familiar with the exhaustion that comes with facing the challenges of a new life.

So I have a clear mission: to help the English-speaking community navigate the beautiful but often complicated French way of life.
I decided to launch Pauline Service because I wanted my work to make sense. I genuinely like people and want to help.
I'm not just a service provider; I'm a member of this community here to make my neighbours' life easier.

I believe in flexibility, not just in my schedule, but in my rates.
Because every situation is unique, I offer flexible rates and tailor-made support to fit your specific project and budget.

I believe in meaningful work that supports our local community and I'll always do it with the same energy and empathy.

So please, don't hesitate to contact me to have a look together at how I could help.
