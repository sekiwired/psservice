About Pauline

With a Franco‑Australian perspective, I bring an extensive knowledge of France intricacies to support the English‑speaking community. Having lived and worked abroad for over two decades, I understand the practical challenges that expats encounter when dealing with French administration. My fluency in both languages enables me to translate documents, clarify procedures, and communicate effectively with all involved.

My professional background includes organising large‑scale events for up to 15 000 people. This experience has strengthened my ability to manage complex logistics, coordinate with different stakeholders, and meet tight deadlines, all while keeping a careful and human eye on details. I bring the same structured approach to everyday tasks.

My goal is to make your life in our Périgord Vert as straightforward and stress‑free as possible, leveraging my organisational skills and familiarity with the area.

If you are looking for reliable responsive support, I would be glad to help. I am flexible in tailoring my services to your needs so please don't hesitate to reach out.
